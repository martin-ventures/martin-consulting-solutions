In Short

AI Visibility is a monthly service from Martin Consulting Solutions. We check whether AI assistants name your business when a customer asks who to hire, fix the facts those assistants read, and report the change against a baseline taken before anything changed.

  • It starts free: a scan of your business and a one-page brief with 3 fixes in order.
  • It is priced in public: $99, $299, or $749 a month, month to month.
  • You can do the first check yourself: the 10-minute guide is free to read, with no sign-up.

What You Get

1You know whether AI names you

We write the questions a customer in your city would ask, put them to 2 AI assistants, and score every answer: accurate, mentioned, inaccurate, or absent.

2You know who gets named instead

Every answer cites its sources. We list the sites that keep appearing, because those are where the assistants learn about businesses like yours.

3The facts get fixed, in order

First your name, city, and services in plain text on your site. Then the same facts in the code that assistants read. Then proof from other people, such as reviews and listings. A person reviews every piece before it is published.

4You see the change on a schedule

We take a baseline before anything changes, then re-ask the same questions on the 1st and the 15th. Your report shows the latest number beside the baseline, with every fix dated so you can see what followed it.

We measure with Meridian, a tool we built for this one job.

What should you expect, honestly?

  • Months 1-3: correct facts when someone asks an assistant about you by name, and zero or one appearance on general questions.
  • Months 4-6: the first appearances on questions that do not use your name.
  • At month 6: if nothing has moved, we say so and change the plan. We do not dress up the numbers.

AI answers vary from one day to the next. That is why we re-ask on a schedule, and why we never promise a ranking.

Can you do this yourself?

Yes, most of it. The 10-minute AI visibility check is free to read. You could hand it to an AI assistant and work through the fixes on your own, and that has real value.

What the monthly plan pays for is doing it every month, measuring it the same way each time, and having a person review the work. We keep the price low because our process and tools are built for this one job, and we pass the savings on.
