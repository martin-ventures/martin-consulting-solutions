How do you check whether AI names your business? The 10-minute AI visibility check

By Jeremy Martin, Martin Consulting Solutions · Published November 17, 2026 · 5 minute read

TL;DR: An AI visibility check tells you whether AI assistants name your business when a customer asks who to hire. Write five customer questions, ask two assistants, and score each answer as accurate, mentioned, inaccurate, or absent. Note who is named instead, fix your plain facts first, then earn reviews and listings. Ask the same questions again on a schedule.

What is an AI visibility check?

An AI visibility check is a short test of whether AI assistants (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) name your business when someone asks a question a customer would ask. Search used to be about ranking on a page. AI answers are about being named at all, and you cannot fix what you have not measured.

The check takes about ten minutes, costs nothing, and any owner can run it.

How do you run the check?

1Write Five Questions

Write the questions a customer would ask an AI assistant before choosing someone like you, such as "Who is the best ___ in my city?" and "Is ___ any good?" Use four questions about your category and one about you by name.

2Ask Two Assistants

Type each question into two AI assistants and save every answer. Use a fresh chat, signed out if you can, so the assistant is not leaning on your history.

3Score Each Answer

For every answer, mark how your business showed up, using the four scores in the next section. Expect a lot of "absent" the first time.

4See Who Is Named Instead

List the sources each answer cites: directories, review sites, forums, and competitors. That is where the assistants learn. The same few sites repeat, and those are your targets.

5Fix in Order

Give the assistants plain facts to read, then proof from other people. The order is in the section below.

6Check Again on a Schedule

Take a baseline before you change anything, then ask the same questions again (we use the 1st and the 15th). Use the same questions every time, or you cannot compare.

How do you score an AI answer?

ScoreWhat It Means
AccurateThe answer names your business and describes it correctly.
MentionedThe answer names your business but says too little to judge.
InaccurateThe answer names your business and gets a fact wrong (e.g., the wrong city, service, or hours).
AbsentThe answer does not name your business.

What should you fix first?

  1. Put your name, city, and services in plain text on your site to give the assistants facts they can read.
  2. Earn reviews on your Google Business Profile to add proof from other people.
  3. Add one listing a month that repeats the same facts to make the name, city, and link consistent everywhere.

What did our own check show?

We ran this check on NextRole, a job search product we own. On October 29, 2026 we asked two AI assistants (Perplexity and Gemini) nine questions a job seeker would ask. NextRole was named on one of the nine: the question that used its name, and both assistants described it correctly. On the other eight it was absent.

That is the number we are working from. The current readings are public, low parts included, on the live demo.

What is the checklist?

  • Five customer questions written down: four about the category, one by name.
  • Each question asked on two assistants in a fresh chat, with every answer saved.
  • Each answer scored: accurate, mentioned, inaccurate, or absent.
  • The sources named instead of you listed.
  • Plain facts fixed on your site first, then reviews, then one listing a month.
  • The same questions asked again on a set date.
